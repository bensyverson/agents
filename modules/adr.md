## Decision records

- **A significant architectural or product decision gets a dated record** in the project's decisions directory (the head names it; `docs/decisions/` is typical), one file per decision.
- **Each record carries four parts**: the context, the options weighed, the decision, and the consequences.
- **A decision that is reversed gets a new record pointing at the old one.** Never edit the history.
- **Link the record from the plan or task that prompted it**, and check the existing records before proposing something that sounds new.
